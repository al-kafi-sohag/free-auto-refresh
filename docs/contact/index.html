Free Auto Refresh

A free, private auto refresh and page monitor extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Features

  • Auto refresh timer for any tab
  • Refresh at random intervals
  • Page monitor with keyword alerts
  • A live countdown on the page
  • Smart pause: it waits for you
  • Keep your place after every refresh
  • Hard reload on a timer
  • Stop on its own, on your terms
  • Profiles, export and import
  • Speaks your language

Use cases

  • Restock alerts for any product page
  • Alerted when a price changes
  • Ticket drops without the manual reload
  • An appointment slot without constant checking
  • An auction to the last second
  • First to know when results are published
  • Told when your status changes
  • Dashboards that stay up to date
  • Watch builds, queues and status pages
  • Zero-setup live reload
  • New job posts as they appear
  • Freelance marketplaces: refresh with care
  • WordPress, without a plugin
  • Live scores that update themselves
  • Price pages current
