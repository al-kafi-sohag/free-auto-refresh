Instagram keeps refreshing? Try this.

A feed that jumps back to the top is frustrating. Some updating is how the app works, but constant reloading points to something you may be able to fix.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Normal update or a fault?

A new feed after you have been away is expected. Reloading every few seconds, jumping while you scroll, or reels restarting over and over suggests a problem with the app, the connection or the phone.

Fixes for the phone app

  1. Check your connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to compare.
  2. Update Instagram and your phone's system.
  3. Force close the app and restart the phone.
  4. On Android, clear Instagram's cache from the phone's app settings.
  5. If it continues, reinstall the app. Make sure you know your login details first.

Fixes for instagram.com

  • Test in a private window to rule out extensions and stored data.
  • Check that no auto refresh timer is running on that tab.
  • In Chrome, keep the site active so the tab is not unloaded.
  • Clear data for instagram.com only, then sign in again.

Do not lose posts you care about

Tap the bookmark icon to save a post or reel, or share it to yourself. On a computer, open the post in its own tab. That is more dependable than trying to stop the feed from changing.

When it is on Instagram's side

If friends see the same thing at the same time, wait and check Instagram's official channels. You can also report the problem from inside the app.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I turn off feed refreshing?

We know of no dependable setting for that. The fixes here deal with unwanted extra reloads.

Will an extension stop it?

Be cautious with any tool that asks for your login. Rule out browser causes first.

Does clearing the cache delete my photos?

Clearing an app's cache removes temporary files, not the posts on your account. Clearing all app data signs you out.
