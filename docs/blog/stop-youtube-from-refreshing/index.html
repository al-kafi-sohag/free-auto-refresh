Stop YouTube reloading by itself.

A video that restarts or a home page that swaps out the thing you were about to click can have several causes. Test them one at a time.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Start with a private window

Open YouTube in a private or incognito window. If it behaves there, the likely cause is an extension or stored data in your normal profile. If it still reloads, look at the browser, the computer or your connection.

Check your extensions

Turn off extensions, then turn them back on one by one. Look closely at anything that changes YouTube, blocks content or refreshes pages. If you use Free Auto Refresh, open it on the YouTube tab and press Stop. The on-page counter shows when it is running.

Stop the tab from being unloaded

If it reloads only when you come back to it, that is probably memory saving. Add youtube.com to the list of sites kept active.

Fix stored data and playback problems

  1. Update your browser.
  2. Clear data for youtube.com.
  3. If video playback crashes the page, try switching hardware acceleration off in browser settings and restart the browser.

Keep videos before the home page changes

The home page is a feed of suggestions, so it can change after any reload. Open interesting videos in new tabs, or add them to Watch later.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I freeze the YouTube home page?

Not with a built-in setting we can recommend. Save videos or open them in new tabs.

Why does the app reload on my phone?

The phone may close it in the background to free memory. Updating the app and restarting the phone can help.

Does a slow connection reload the page?

A slow connection mostly causes buffering. A full page reload usually has another cause.
