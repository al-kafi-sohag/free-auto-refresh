Auto refresh in Firefox.

Firefox does not include a way to reload a tab on a timer. An add-on is the practical answer, and here are the few things you can do without one.

Al Kafi SohagNov 18, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Method 1: use an add-on

  1. Open the Free Auto Refresh page for Firefox and click Add to Firefox.
  2. Open the page you want to reload.
  3. Click the toolbar icon, choose an interval, and press Start.

Each tab keeps its own timer, and Alt + Shift + R starts or stops it.

Method 2: no add-on, manual only

Without an add-on you can only reload by hand: F5 for a normal reload, or Ctrl + Shift + R to bypass the cache. Firefox has no hidden setting to repeat it for you.

What the add-on adds

  • Random intervals instead of a fixed beat.
  • Page monitor: stop and notify when text appears or the content changes.
  • Limits: stop after a time or a number of refreshes.

Stopping unwanted refreshes in Firefox

Firefox can block pages that refresh themselves. In about:config, set accessibility.blockautorefresh to true. This is separate from any add-on.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does Firefox have a built-in auto refresh?

No. You need an add-on to reload a tab on a timer.

Is Free Auto Refresh free on Firefox?

Yes. It is free, with no account and no paid tier.

Can I refresh more than one tab?

Yes. Each tab has its own timer, so one fast tab never affects the others.
