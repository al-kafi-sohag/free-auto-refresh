Price drop alerts in Chrome.

You do not always need a full price tracker. For one product you care about, watching the price on the page itself is enough.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

How it works

The extension reloads the product page and compares one part of it, the price, with the last check. If it changes, refreshing stops and you get a notification.

Set it up in four steps

  1. Open the product page and the Free Auto Refresh popup.
  2. Go to Advanced settings, Watch the page and choose Stop when content changes.
  3. In the selector field, enter the CSS selector of the price element, for example #price or .price. Right-click the price, choose Inspect, and read the element's id or class.
  4. Pick an interval of a few minutes and press Start.

Why limit it to the price element

Watching a single element avoids false alarms from banners, countdown clocks and recommended items. Only the price can trigger the alert.

Know the limits

  • It alerts you to a change, up or down. It does not draw a price history chart.
  • The browser must stay open on that tab.
  • It does not apply coupons or buy for you.

When you need a real tracker

For price history over months, or many products at once, a dedicated tracker is the better tool. For one watch, this is faster. More in the price tracking guide.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does it show price history?

No. It tells you when the price changes, not how it moved over time.

How do I find the CSS selector?

Right-click the price, choose Inspect, and copy the element's id or class.

Can I watch several products?

Yes, one tab each. Each tab has its own timer and watch rule.
