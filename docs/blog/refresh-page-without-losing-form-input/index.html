How-to

Refresh a page without losing your typing.

Nothing is worse than writing a long message and watching the page reload. If you need a page to refresh and also need to use it, there is a setting for exactly that.

Al Kafi SohagNov 19, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Why you lose your text

A reload loads the page again from the server. Anything you typed in a field that was not saved or sent is gone. Some sites keep a draft, many do not.

Turn on Smart pause

Open Advanced settings and switch on Smart pause. The extension holds back a reload while you are typing in a form field, and carries on after you stop. You can also have it hold when you click or scroll, and choose whether a click restarts the countdown or stops it.

Add scroll position for long pages

Turn on Keep scroll position. Each reload then returns to the same place on the page instead of jumping to the top.

Safer habits

  • Do not run a timer on a page where you write long text, like an email or a document. Use Smart pause only as a safety net.
  • Press Alt + Shift + R to stop the timer while you do a longer task.
  • Copy long text before pressing Send, if the site might time out.

Where this is useful

It fits support queues where you reply to tickets while the list refreshes, WordPress previews, order forms on a page you are also watching, and booking pages where you fill in details while waiting for a slot.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does Smart pause save my text?

No. It stops the reload while you are typing, so the text is never lost. It does not store anything.

What if I stop typing but leave the field open?

The timer carries on after you stop typing, so finish and send before pausing for long.

Can I pause on click or scroll too?

Yes. It is optional, and a click or scroll can either restart the countdown or stop refreshing.
