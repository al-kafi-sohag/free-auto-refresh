See new job listings before the rush.

Good jobs often get dozens of applicants within hours. If a board or a company careers page has no alerts of its own, a page you check by hand is easy to miss. Here is a calmer way.

Al Kafi SohagNov 19, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Sort by newest first

Open the search results you care about and sort them by newest or date posted. Save that page as a bookmark. New jobs appear at the top, so a change at the top of the list is the signal to watch.

Set up the watch

  1. Open the sorted results page and the Free Auto Refresh popup.
  2. Under Advanced settings, Watch the page, choose Stop when the page changes.
  3. If the page has ads or a changing banner, pick the results list with a CSS selector so only a new job counts.
  4. Set an interval of 5 to 15 minutes and press Start.

When the list changes, the extension stops, shows a notification and flashes the tab title.

Company career pages work too

Many small employers have a careers page with no alert option. Watch it for the text "No open positions" and choose Stop when text disappears. You will see the page when the line goes away.

What it does not do

It does not apply for you, send your CV or sign you in. You read the job and decide. Some boards do not allow automated page loading, so read the site's terms and keep the interval gentle. Five to fifteen minutes is plenty for a job board.

Tidy habits

  • Use a separate saved profile for each job search.
  • Add a time limit so the tab does not run overnight.
  • Stay signed in, and keep the computer awake while it watches.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will it apply to jobs for me?

No. It tells you when a page changed. You do everything else yourself.

How often should it check a job board?

Every 5 to 15 minutes. Jobs are not posted every few seconds.

Why did it trigger when no job was added?

Ads or a 'viewed by' counter changed. Watch only the results list with a CSS selector.
