Stop losing your place on Facebook.

You find an interesting post, glance away, and the feed reloads. Facebook does not offer a simple switch to stop its feed updating, but you can rule out other causes and keep what you find.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Keep the post before it disappears

  • On a computer: open the post in a new tab by Ctrl-clicking (Cmd-clicking on a Mac) its date or time link.
  • On any device: use the post's menu and choose Save post.
  • For Marketplace: open the listing and save it instead of relying on scrolling back.

On a computer: rule out your browser

  1. Try a private window. If the problem stops, suspect an extension or stored data.
  2. Check that no auto refresh timer is running on the Facebook tab.
  3. In Chrome, add facebook.com to the sites Memory Saver keeps active.
  4. If it still misbehaves, clear data for facebook.com only. You will need to sign in again.

In the phone app

The app may load a new feed after you have been away, especially if the phone closed it to free memory. Update the app, free up memory and storage, and restart the phone. On Android you can also clear the app's cache from your phone's app settings.

What not to rely on

Be careful with apps or extensions that promise to freeze your feed or ask for your Facebook password. They need broad access, and sites change often. Check what to look for before installing an extension.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is there a setting to turn off Facebook auto refresh?

We know of no dependable setting that stops the feed from updating. Saving posts is the reliable workaround.

Does Free Auto Refresh refresh Facebook by itself?

No. It only reloads a tab where you press Start, unless you turned on Remember this page for it.

Why does it happen when I switch apps?

The phone may have closed the app in the background to free memory, so it starts fresh when you return.
