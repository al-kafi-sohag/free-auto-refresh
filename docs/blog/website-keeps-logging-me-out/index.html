Troubleshooting

Why you keep getting signed out.

Being logged out once in a while is normal security. Being logged out every few minutes, or every time you reopen the browser, usually has a cause you can find.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

One site or all of them?

If every site signs you out, look at your browser and computer. If only one does, look at that site's settings, your account and its own timeout rules.

Check cookie settings

  • Make sure your browser is not set to delete site data when you close it.
  • Check that the site is not blocked from saving cookies.
  • Remember that private windows forget you when you close them.
  • Cleaner apps and privacy extensions can delete cookies too.

Other common causes

  • A wrong date or time on your computer.
  • A VPN or network that keeps changing your address, which some sites treat as risky.
  • Signing in somewhere else, on services that allow only one active session.
  • Damaged site data. Clear data for that one site and sign in fresh.

If you are signed out unexpectedly and see activity you do not recognise, change your password and review your account's security page.

Session timeouts set by the site

Many sites end a session after a period of inactivity, and some end it after a fixed time no matter what you do. Look for a Keep me signed in option when you log in.

Can auto refresh keep a session alive?

On some sites a reload counts as activity. On many it does not, and it can never extend a fixed time limit. Do not use it to get around a security timeout on banking, work, school, health or shared computers. Those limits protect you, and your organisation's rules may forbid bypassing them. For harmless sites where it is allowed, use a gentle interval of several minutes and a time limit so it does not run all night.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does clearing my cache sign me out?

Clearing cookies and site data does. Clearing only cached images and files normally does not.

Why am I signed out after closing the browser?

A setting may be deleting site data on close, or the site only keeps a temporary session unless you ask it to remember you.

Is it safe to stay signed in?

On a private, locked device it is usually reasonable. On a shared computer, sign out when you finish.
