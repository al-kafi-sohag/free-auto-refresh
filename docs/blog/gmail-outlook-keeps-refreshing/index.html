Troubleshooting

Inbox that keeps reloading?

There are two different problems here: an inbox that reloads when you do not want it to, and wanting new mail to show up without pressing refresh.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Fix an inbox that keeps reloading

  1. Open your mail in a private window. If it works there, suspect an extension or stored data.
  2. Disable extensions that touch email, then re-enable them one at a time.
  3. Make sure no auto refresh timer is running on the mail tab.
  4. Clear data for the mail site only and sign in again.
  5. Update your browser. For a work or school account, ask your administrator if it still happens.

Do you need to auto refresh your inbox?

Usually not. Gmail and Outlook on the web are built to show new mail while they are open. A forced reload can interrupt a draft or close the message you are reading.

Get notified instead

  • Gmail: in Settings, look for desktop notifications and turn on the option you want.
  • Outlook on the web: look for notification options in Settings.
  • Allow notifications for the site in your browser, and check that your computer's do-not-disturb mode is off.

If new mail really is not appearing

First check your filters, spam folder and other inbox tabs. Then see whether the browser has put the tab to sleep, and keep it active. If you still choose to use a timer on a plain webmail page that does not update, pick several minutes and leave pause while typing on.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will auto refresh make email arrive faster?

No. It only reloads the page. Delivery happens on the mail servers.

Why does my draft disappear?

A reload can close the compose window. Drafts are often saved automatically, so check the Drafts folder.

Does this apply to the desktop Outlook app?

No. A browser extension can only act on pages open in the browser.
