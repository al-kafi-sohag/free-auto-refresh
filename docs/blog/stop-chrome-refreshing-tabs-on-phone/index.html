Stop Chrome reloading tabs on your phone.

You hop to another app to copy something, come back, and Chrome reloads the page. You can make it happen less often, but be ready for an honest answer about stopping it completely.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Why it happens

Phones have limited memory. When another app needs some, the system or Chrome can drop a background tab. Returning to it means downloading the page again. A page can also reload itself, which has a different fix.

On Android

  1. Close tabs and apps you are not using.
  2. Update Chrome and Android.
  3. Open your phone's Settings, find Chrome in the app list, and look at its Battery options. Allowing unrestricted background use may help on some phones.
  4. Free up storage and restart the phone.

Names differ between phone makers, and none of this can guarantee that a tab stays loaded.

On iPhone and iPad

iOS manages memory itself, and there is no setting that keeps Chrome tabs alive. Closing unused tabs and apps, updating, and restarting are the practical steps. See also why Safari keeps reloading pages, since many of the same ideas apply.

Protect what you are doing

  • Draft long messages in a notes app.
  • Take a screenshot of confirmation numbers.
  • Save articles for offline reading when Chrome offers it.
  • Finish forms and checkouts without switching apps if you can.

On a computer it is easier

Desktop Chrome has a Memory Saver setting with a list of sites to keep active. Read how to keep a tab active. Note that Free Auto Refresh runs in desktop browsers, not in Chrome on phones.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is there a Chrome flag that stops mobile reloads?

Experimental flags come and go and are not a dependable fix. They can also cause other problems.

Does incognito mode stop reloading?

No. Memory limits apply to incognito tabs too.

Why does one site reload even when I stay on it?

That is usually the page itself, an ad on it, or a crash. Try another browser to compare.
