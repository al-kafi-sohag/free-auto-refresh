Safari keeps reloading on your iPhone?

You are reading, the screen blinks, and you are back at the top. Or you switch apps for a moment and the tab loads from scratch. Here is how to narrow it down.

Al Kafi SohagNov 27, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Work out which kind of reload it is

  • When you come back to a tab: the iPhone probably freed memory and has to load the page again.
  • While you are reading, on one site only: the page itself is likely refreshing, or it is too heavy and crashing.
  • On every site: look at Safari, your extensions or the iPhone itself.

Quick fixes to try first

  1. Close tabs you no longer need.
  2. Close the Safari app and open it again, then restart the iPhone.
  3. Update iOS in Settings > General > Software Update.
  4. Check that you have some free storage.

If it is only one site

Delete that site's stored data: open Settings, find Safari (under Apps on newer versions), then Advanced > Website Data. Our guide to clearing cache for one website covers this. Also try Reader view, or turn off content blockers and Safari extensions for a test. If a message says a problem repeatedly occurred, the page is failing to load and the site owner may need to fix it.

Can you stop Safari unloading tabs?

There is no switch that forces Safari to keep every tab loaded. To protect your work, write long text in a notes app and paste it in when it is ready, and bookmark your place before switching to another app.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is my iPhone broken if tabs reload?

Not usually. Reloading a background tab after using other apps is normal. Constant reloads on every site, even after a restart and update, are worth taking to support.

Will Private Browsing help?

It is a useful test. If a site works there, stored data or an extension may be the problem.

Why does it happen with many tabs open?

Each active page uses memory. When memory runs short, the system can unload pages you are not looking at.
