Catch an appointment slot when it opens.

Booking pages for tests, clinics and offices often show no free slots, then release a few when someone cancels. Here is a calm way to be first to see one.

Al Kafi SohagNov 18, 20262 min read
Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Find the message that changes

A booking page usually shows a line such as "No appointments available" or "No slots". When a slot opens, that line goes away or changes. That is the signal to watch.

Set up the watch

  1. Open the booking page and the Free Auto Refresh popup.
  2. Go to Advanced settings, Watch the page and choose Stop when text disappears.
  3. Type the exact "no slots" wording as it appears on the page.
  4. Choose an interval of 1 to 3 minutes, turn on notifications, and press Start.

Mind the page load

Booking pages can load slowly. Keep Wait for the page to load on, and add a second or two of Extra wait so a slot is not missed or falsely reported.

Be polite and stay within the rules

  • Do not refresh every second. Cancellations are not that frequent, and fast refreshing can get your connection blocked.
  • Some booking systems forbid automation. Read the terms of the site first.
  • It does not book for you. You still choose the slot and confirm it yourself.

Keep the tab ready

The alert only works while the browser is open on that page. Stay signed in, and do not let the computer sleep. The appointment slots guide has the full settings.

Questions, answered.

Will it book the slot for me?

No. It alerts you and you complete the booking.

How often should it check?

Every 1 to 3 minutes is enough for most booking pages.

Is it allowed?

It depends on the site. Read its terms, and keep the interval gentle.
