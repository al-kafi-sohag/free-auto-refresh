Privacy

The short version: the extension collects nothing and sends nothing.

The extension

It reads the visible text of a page only in the tab where you turned on page watching, and only inside your browser. It never records what you type. Settings, profiles, presets and your language choice are stored locally. If you switch on Remember this page, the address of that page is stored locally too, so it can start again later; pressing Stop removes it. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Storage saves your settings.
  • Notifications shows an alert when a watch rule matches.
  • Website access lets the timer and counter run on the page you choose.

This website

It uses Microsoft Clarity on every visit to see how the pages are used (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement). Clarity sets cookies only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. Google Analytics, which counts visits, also loads only after Accept. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

The contact form sends what you type (your message, and your name and email if you give them) to the developer through Telegram, so he can read and answer it. It is not stored on this website and is not used for anything else.

The full policy is in PRIVACY.md on GitHub.
