Privacy

The short version: the extension collects nothing and sends nothing.

The extension

It reads the visible text of a page only in the tab where you turned on page watching, and only inside your browser. It never records what you type. Settings, profiles and your language choice are stored locally. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Storage saves your settings.
  • Notifications shows an alert when a watch rule matches.
  • Website access lets the timer and counter run on the page you choose.

This website

It loads Google Analytics to count visits, and only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

The full policy is in PRIVACY.md on GitHub.
