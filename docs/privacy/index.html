Privacy

The extension

It reads the visible text of a page only in the tab where you turned on page watching, and only inside your browser. It never records what you type. Settings, profiles and your language choice are stored locally. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

Storage saves your settings. Notifications show an alert when a watch rule matches. Website access lets the timer and counter run on the page you choose.

This website

It loads Google Analytics to count visits, and only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

The full policy is in PRIVACY.md on GitHub.

Free Auto Refresh

A free, private auto refresh and page monitor extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Features

  • Auto refresh timer for any tab
  • Refresh at random intervals
  • Page monitor with keyword alerts
  • A live countdown on the page
  • Smart pause: it waits for you
  • Keep your place after every refresh
  • Hard reload on a timer
  • Stop on its own, on your terms
  • Profiles, export and import
  • Speaks your language
