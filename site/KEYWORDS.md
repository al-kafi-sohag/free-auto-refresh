These people want a keyboard shortcut, not an extension. A good guide page (shortcuts for Chrome, Firefox, Mac, Windows,
plus "do it automatically on a timer") can bring traffic, but few will install. Worth one strong page, not more.

Dropped: retailer names (Walmart, Target, Best Buy and others: people want that shop's own checker), Keepa and Amazon price history,
and all Shopify / WooCommerce "back in stock" terms (shop owners looking for a plugin, not shoppers).

Note on Amazon Relay: truck drivers refresh the load board. Amazon's rules forbid automated refreshing there and accounts are
suspended for it. Same position as freelance marketplaces: do not build a page that encourages it.

## What to change on the website

1. **Home and Chrome page titles:** lead with "Auto Refresh" + "Chrome Extension"; work in "auto reload", "page refresher" and "free".
2. **New guide pages:** "How to auto refresh a page (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)", "How to hard refresh (all shortcuts)", "How to stop a page auto refreshing".
3. **New browser pages:** Edge, Brave, Opera.
4. **Page monitor page:** retitle around "website change detection" and "track website changes", which have 10x the volume of "keyword alert".
5. **Use-case pages worth keeping:** restock, price, tickets, appointments. The others have no measured volume yet.
6. **Possible comparison pages:** Easy Auto Refresh alternative, Auto Refresh Plus alternative.
